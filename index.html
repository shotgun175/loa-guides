Extreme Mordum Mech and pattern deltas

What changes from the Act 3 Mordum you already run, from Memorizer's guide: a commentary run over a Korean Nightmare clear. Getting hit fills your gauge by a lot and a full gauge wipes the raid, puddles stay for 8 minutes, after the 2nd stage break the jumper can ask for A or D, and Nightmare has no revives.

Read this first. This is a difference guide, not a full walkthrough: Memorizer covers what changes from the original Act 3 Mordum in the system and the mechs, plus a few patterns. He could not clear Extreme Nightmare himself, so most of the footage is Sotimerry's Korean clear, used with permission. For a full run with every pattern, watch Sotimerry's clear.

No Nightmare in Russia. Russia's official preview (11 November 2026), written while the content was still being tested, gives this raid only Normal at 1730 and Hard at 1770, with unlimited revives on both, from 18 November to 16 December 2026. Russia dropped Nightmare from both Extreme Act 3 and its Extreme Finale, saying adjustments and balance changes didn't fix problems with the raid's implementation. The West's roadmap gives only November and says more details will come closer to release.

Prep

Shorthand used on this page, the system changes, what to bring and when to spend sidereal.

Just Guard

Written JG, always in yellow. A tap of G on the cue. Memorizer just says guard.

x475

HP bar line, as Memorizer labels each mech. His footage often shows the bar lower than the label, and the video doesn't explain the gap.

Red and blue

Players are red or blue, and several mechs care which. Written in plain words here, never in the call colors.

Gauge

Your own gauge. Getting hit fills it. At half your color flips and you get a puddle. Full is a raid wipe.

Puddle

The ground marker you drop off. In Extreme it stays for 8 minutes, so where you drop it matters.

Jumper

The jump pad. After the 2nd stage break the key can be A or D, not just A. Check the prompt before you press.

Stage break

At x375, x250 and 125~ on his slide; the chapters also name a 4th. Maxroll calls them Break & Duel. Puddles dropped on the 1st break's side are removed by it, and later puddles go to the next break's side.

+3 and +3+1

Sidereal cost. A cast spends 3 of your 5 bars. The +1 is the follow-up cast, which does something different, for 1 more bar.

Battle items

  • Atropine Potion . Written "Atro" on Memorizer's list.
  • Dark . Written "dark" on his list; the video doesn't give the full name.
  • Splendid Sacred Charm . The third item. The video gives no timings for any of them.

Gauge

  • Fills a lot . Getting hit by patterns fills it by a lot, compared to the original.
  • At half . Your color reverses and you get a puddle. In his example, paused just after the hit, it was 6 seconds out.
  • Full . Raid wipe. It is why x425's inner circle can't be greeded any more.

Puddles

  • 8 minutes . Up from 2 in the original, per his notes.
  • Where . On the side with the single green orb on the minimap. That is where the 1st stage break happens, and the break removes them.
  • After that . His notes: "Put 1st stage break -> then next". At x140 they go to the side of the next stage break.

Jumper

  • After the 2nd break . The key can be A or D, not just A. Check it first.
  • Where he calls it . x200, Supernova #2 and Tracking Laser.
  • Chained players . Can't take the jumper at all (Tracking Laser).

Hyper awakening

  • x275 anvil . Don't use it. Save it for a harder mech later on.
  • Dance Floor . Use it on the third zigzag instead of guarding.

One shots

What the video calls a raid wipe, a one-shot or a kill. Nightmare has no revives.

Raid wipe. Getting hit by patterns fills your gauge by a lot in Extreme.

Fine in the original. In Extreme it fills your gauge to full: raid wipe.

No rectangle telegraph. Backing away from the yellow-puddle player can walk you into it, and it kills.

Raid wipe. Players whose color is opposite the big orb's walk in front of the boss and kill it; the big orb's color takes the jumper.

Dodge it. Getting hit is a guaranteed death by falling.

Changed in Extreme Memorizer 1:49Sotimerry 0:51

  • Others: on the boss

The lightning spins really fast, and so does the boss's beam. People get a mark three times, randomly, and the lightning that spreads from the marked player zaps people.

  1. Marked player: go to the outer edge and face the edge. The lightning then spreads in an X shape. Stay right at the edge and the outer lightning passes you too.

1st stage break

Changed in Extreme Memorizer 5:14Sotimerry 5:10
  • Stagger
  • Puddles to the break

Three players get a puddle, one gets the mark, which also gives a cross-shaped lightning, and the rest have nothing.

x160stagger

Hammer Split with random changing area

Changed in Extreme Memorizer 9:26Memorizer 10:04Sotimerry 13:42
  • Stagger
  • Balthorr +3+1
  • Swap colors

Stagger the boss and ignore the hammer. For sidereal you can use Bastion, as in the original, or Balthorr on the boss: once the stagger is done, the lead uses the +1 right away while the boss is still staggered. That does a lot of damage and lowers the DPS everyone needs, so Balthorr +3+1 is normally the pick over Bastion.

After the stagger, unlike the original, the changing area goes to a random person, not to whoever is closest to the boss. That makes it easy to brain-fart. His on-screen solve:

  1. Different colors gather to the boss first.
  2. Correct color gets it: that player goes to the nearest opposite color. The people who need to change follow, and all swap.
  3. Different color gets it: as in the original, the different colors all swap together in front of the boss.

Last Stand with timed line walks

Lethal lines Memorizer 13:40Sotimerry 20:07
  • Bastion +3+1
  • Your color
  • Berserk timer

It opens the same way as the original raid.

  1. Zigzag puddle: dodge it and drop the puddles on the backside.
  2. Beaming cross: three times, for a long time.
  3. First: dodge it right after it ends, because it fires once more.
  4. Second: stand in the safe zone, then move when it ends. Use Bastion +3+1.
  5. Third: dodge it, then move one time.
  6. Color: the boss turns red, so every blue player changes color. Anyone who doesn't will pretty much die. The boss can be blue as well. It changes once more; in the footage it went neutral yellow, so that one didn't matter.
  7. Lines: lethal. Everyone gathers, then walks out when the berserk timer (the countdown at the left end of the boss bar) hits exactly one, 1:00 on the timer in the footage. The lines come once more: this time move at exactly 54 seconds.
  8. After that the pattern just repeats. Full DPS.

Fails People can die if they don't gather properly for the lines.
