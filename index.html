Extreme Brelshaza Mech and pattern deltas

Everything below is what changes from the Act 2 Brelshaza you already run. The base fight is unchanged in shape: same phases, same colors, same team meter. Extreme adds a new mech at x400, bolts extra attacks onto patterns you already know, and turns several of them into instant deaths.

Difficulties 3Normal, Hard, Nightmare. Guide is filmed on Nightmare.
Life counts 0Nightmare has none. Normal and Hard get one extra.

Read this first. This is a difference guide, not a full walkthrough. It assumes you can already clear Act 2 Brelshaza and know the colors, the team meter, the clone stagger and the orbs. The guide author rates it well above Aegir. If you have never run the original, watch the original Act 2 guide before this one.

Prep

Shorthand used throughout, then what to bring and when to spend sidereal.

Just Guard

Written JG, always in yellow here. The perfect-timing block.

Counter

Blue. Counters have no internal cooldown and each one feeds sidereal gauge, so take every single one.

Co-op counter

Purple. Needs two players on it together.

3x and 3x+1

Clock positions. 3x is 12, 3, 6, 9. 3x+1 is 1, 4, 7, 10.

+3 and +3+1

Sidereal cost. A cast spends 3 of your 5 bars. The +1 is the follow-up cast, which does something different, for 1 more bar.

Puddle

The ground marker you drop off. Called a portal in the Korean guide, same object.

Battle items

  • HP potions . Your choice.
  • Time Stop Potion . The clean answer to the crossing lines during Glass break. Track its cooldown, that mech repeats every 3 minutes.
  • Dark Grenade . Damage buff for 20 seconds, or 25 seconds on the Splendid version.
  • Splendid Sacred Charm or Bomb . Either one. Same job, different delivery: the charm works on close proximity, the bomb can be thrown. Shield for the clone imprison at x400 and again in the Phase 1 clone pattern.

Engravings and cards

  • Artist, Bard . Awakening, Magick Stream, Drops of Ether, Expert, Heavy Armor.
  • Paladin, Valkyrie . Awakening, Magick Stream, Drops of Ether, Expert, then one of VPH (Vital Point Hit), CE (Crisis Evasion) or EE (Explosive Expert).
  • Cards . Lightning set, she is Demon type and weak to Lightning.
  • Of those three . The Korean guide calls out Crisis Evasion for supports specifically.

Support awakenings

  • Phase 1 . The x400 stagger mech, and the Black hole mech.
  • Phase 2 . Every half gauge mech.
  • Phase 3 . Hyper awakening at the Scythe mech. Awakening at every Double JG in center.
  • Phase 4 . Every full gauge mech.

Support jobs

  • Eat the opening puddle . Stand in it with push immunity so a support carries the meteor debuff.
  • Watch the stragglers . During the twister pattern, keep eyes on whoever is late to the safe zone.
  • Tank the Scythe beam . After hyper awakening, stand outside the safe spot and eat it with push immunity so the safe spot stays roomy.

Positions

  • x335 colors . 3x+1 positions, same as the original.
  • x145 freeze . 3x and 3x+1 positions.
  • Scythe guard . P1 goes top and left, P2 goes bottom and right. Same as the original raid.
  • Double JG . Drop puddles at the bottom, then JG from the top.

Carry at least 2 bars into Phase 4 or you cannot trigger special Azena at all. Unlike the original, do not save Nineveh for the crystals: in extreme you break them with your own skills and spend Azena repeatedly to push the phase.

One shots

Six things in this raid end a run outright. On Nightmare there is no life count to absorb them.

Glass break, final JG

Failed guard

4 million damage on a failed guard. The hardest mech in the raid and it repeats every 3 minutes in Phase 3.

Phase 4 final guard

No outer ice break

Fail it and you die. Time Stop and hyper awakening do not work in this phase, so the JG is the only answer.

x145

Five frozen, raid wipe

More than four frozen players wipes the raid. One party freezes on purpose, the other hides.

Scythe guard

Freeze outside the safe spot

Freezing inside is survivable, outside is fatal. If the designated guard player freezes, the scythe can kill the whole raid.

Double JG in center

Fall death

Fail the guard while standing away from her and you get knocked off the platform. Stick to her body.

Phase 3 whirlwind swings

JG, then JG or counter

Guard after the first swing, then a JG or co-op counter after the third. Failing either one kills.

Supports use awakening. Players get puddles: drop them outside, they silence anyone who touches them.

The arena is four quadrants. A white icon lights up in three of them, in sequence. Move through the quadrants in the order they lit up.

Twister

New in Extreme Chapter "Regular patterns" / 3:25
  • Spread then stack
  • Massive damage

Comes around roughly every 35 seconds on top of the usual find-the-real-one pattern.

  1. Dodge the tornado attack, three times.
  2. Players with the snowflake circle gather in front of the boss. That generates the safe zone.
  3. Everyone else drops their puddle outside, then stacks into the safe zone.
  4. Dodge the final donut and the pattern ends.

Take your 3x and 3x+1 positions. More than four frozen players wipes the raid, so the solve is deliberate: one party freezes, the other party hides. Decide which party is which before the pull.

Phase 3 / below x145
3 minrepeats

Glass break

Hardest mech Chapter "Glass break" / 5:06
  • Time Stop
  • Slow JG
  • One-shot kill

First one lands after the first pattern of Phase 3, then repeats every 3 minutes. This is the mech that decides your prog.

  1. Dodge the glass.
  2. Time Stop the crossing lines. They imprison for 3 seconds and clutter your view.
  3. Dodge the remaining lines on the field. These do massive damage.
  4. Slow JG at the end. The cue is when the pattern at the edge disappears.

Fails The final guard is a one-shot kill, 4 million damage. You can hyper awaken into her to ignore it instead, but the guard itself is easy once you know the cue.

No Time Stop If it is on cooldown, get caught by the crossing line early on purpose. You get freed before the bottom lines arrive. Getting caught late is what kills you.

Scythe guard

Chapter "Scythe guard" / 6:22
  • Hyper awakening
  • JG
  • Freeze chain

Comes a few seconds after Glass break, so treat the pair as one long mech. Supports hyper awaken the moment it starts.

  1. Take your side. P1 goes top and left, P2 goes bottom and right, same as the original.
  2. Ice puddles freeze anyone they touch, so do not stack until you are in the safe spot.
  3. The designated guard player takes the edge. Everyone else gives them room.
  4. A beam crosses the safe spot at the end. Dodge it.

Fails Freezing inside the safe spot is fine. Freezing outside it kills. If the guard player freezes, the scythe can kill everyone.

Support tech After hyper awakening, supports can stand outside the safe spot and tank the beam with push-immune skills. That leaves the safe spot wide open and cuts accidental freezes.
Damage check Strong parties spend Gustaven +3 here.

Final guard

Chapter "Team meter (After 0 bar)" / 8:59
  • JG, no outs
  • Shields
  • Fail equals death

Supports use awakening here. Fail this guard and you die. Time Stop and hyper awakening do not work in this phase.

Timing: JG when the circle reaches the edge.

Easier than the original The original had a debuff that stopped you guarding twice in a row, which forced the guard plus hyper awakening dance. Extreme drops it. Since hyper awakening and Time Stop do nothing here anyway, you simply JG every time the pattern comes up.

Also A player gets chosen and explodes several times during this. That is the other reason supports awaken here. Without a shield up, the explosions alone can kill.

Regular patterns

The regular attack rotation, phase by phase. Counter patterns have no internal cooldown and every counter feeds sidereal gauge, so take all of them.

Phase 1

Clones Splendid Charm

They imprison you. Pop the charm when they appear.

Lines

Move out when the lines appear. The red telegraph that shows up alongside them is a bluff, it does no damage.

Meteors then land on whoever is carrying the meteor debuff.

Line hit debuff Cleanse

Getting clipped by a line gives an uncleansable debuff. After you cast five skills it converts into a silence, and that version can be cleansed. Call for it.

Puddles Never touch

Touching one gives the meteor debuff and triggers a large donut AoE for heavy damage. Stop DPS and dodge it out.

Tentacles

She strikes the spot where there is no tentacle. It binds you and hits hard.

Stand right on top of her and the follow-up guard attack misses you entirely. Stay close, keep DPSing, and push-immune the attack coming from the floor.

Front charge Counter

She charges forward and leaves puddles behind. A clone turns to face Brelshaza, that is your counter.

Push Counter

After the push, the clone wants the counter taken from her backside.

Pizza Counter

Darkness puddles drop and the pizza goes out. The clone counter is on the second pizza. Take a shield from your support and burn damage through it.

Left front Counter

The clone wants this counter from her left front. It knocks down, so have push immunity ready.

Phase 2

Dark orbs

The orbs suck you in. The puddles they leave silence you, so drop them away from the group.

Inner slam

After the second guard she hits the inner side and it hurts. Move out, or push-immune it with a support shield.

Turn attack Counter

After the first attack, the next one is counterable. She can turn, so if you end up on that side be ready to take it.

Guard swap JG

After the attack she wants a JG instead of a counter. This one catches people running the original's muscle memory.

Two guards, not three JG

The sequence is shortened to two guards. Dodge to the side while guarding and stay in.

Phase 3

Whirlwind swings Lethal

After the first swing, JG. After the third swing, it is either a JG or a co-op counter. Fail either one and it kills.

Everyone, aggro holder included, moves with her and stays in front.

Ice twisters New

She draws lines that freeze into ice, then the ice becomes twisters that chase players. Move all the way out immediately, then dodge the twister.

Not the same thing as the Phase 2 twister pattern.

Hand tell

After the orbs are destroyed, read the hand. Right hand raised, she hits the inner side. Left hand, she hits the outer side.

Crescent JG

She attacks twice, then fires a crescent. If she turns back and shoots, another attack follows that needs a JG. It does massive damage and can knock you off to your death.

Easiest answer: go behind her and ignore the whole thing.
