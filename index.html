Lost Ark / Belgardin / Gates 1 and 2

Belgardin Mech and pattern guide

A full walkthrough of both gates, built from Korean Nightmare footage ahead of the NA release. Gate 1 is a stack-management fight on a 10 minute timer. Gate 2 is a JG-heavy fight on a crumbling floor, where most deaths are falls. Both gates have a Rumble (Brawl in the client), a burst phase with its own 3-step bar.

Prep

Shorthand used throughout, then what to bring and the systems that run under every mech.

Just Guard

Written JG, always in yellow. A tap of G on the cue.

Holding guard

New in this raid; the client calls it Holding Just Guard. Dashed yellow. Hold G facing the boss until it fills. Usually leads into a counter or a second guard.

Counter

Blue. A normal one-player counter.

Co-op counter

Purple. Needs 2, 3 or 4 players together; the number is always given.

x240

HP bar line. The mech is due when the boss bar reaches that multiplier. Mechs can fire late if the line passes during another phase.

Rumble

Brawl in the client (KR 대난투), also called the Battle Phase. A burst phase with a 3-step bar and its own guards. Not tied to an HP line. The boss counts as staggered during it.

Pizza

A pie-slice AoE. Clock positions name the slice: 5 means the 5 o'clock slice.

3x and 3x+1

Clock positions. 3x is 12, 3, 6, 9. 3x+1 is 1, 4, 7, 10.

Spacebar check

Mindreader in the client. A screen glitch warns it is coming; dodge on the cue. Success shows Insight (통찰). Missing it gives Confusion, which inverts your mouse for the follow-up; entering the Rumble clears it. In Gate 1 these start at x240.

Battle items

  • Splendid Dark Grenade . Everyone carries them for the relay below.
  • Atropine Potion . Gate 1: pop it at the start of the x195 bar. Gate 2: long groggy windows and the Rumble.
  • Time Stop . Gate 1 alternative to Atropine. Dodges the ghost-coffin floor attack.
  • Sacred Charm . Supports, Gate 1. On Agam's list and the cheat sheet's support row.
  • Last slot: Serca item . Shadow of Corvus Tul Rak in the client. Swap to the Belgardin item once unlocked (next card).

Belgardin battle item

  • Unlock . Succeed at the Gate 2 x320 add mech once. That grants the achievement and the recipe.
  • What it is . A stronger flame grenade: the Serca recipe with a flame grenade in place of the whirlwind grenade. Phaetus An Kragma's Shadow in the client; roster-bound, usable from 1750.
  • Where to throw it . G1 x75 into the center (hits all three clones). G1 and G2 Rumble. G2 x320 small adds and the x200 stagger.

Party setup

  • Dark Grenade relay . Set a throw order. The next player throws as the previous 25 second debuff ends and calls it. Full uptime for the whole fight.
  • Broken Bone + Domination . Gate 2 only. About 2 players on the Broken Bone relic book plus the Domination stat. The boss is JG'd, countered and staggered constantly, so it rarely falls off.
  • Push immunity . Gate 1: worth a lot, almost every hit knocks down. Gate 2: JG attacks ignore it, so guard instead of tanking.

Gate 1 systems

  • Permanent debuff . Certain hits add a stack that never expires. 6 kills on Nightmare, 10 on Hard. Normal has none per the cheat sheet, though Memorizer gave 10 for Normal too. Each pattern below says if it stacks.
  • x195 attack debuff . Separate stack during that mech only. Nightmare: -10% attack per hit, 7 stacks and you are stuck until the mech ends. Hard: -5% per hit, 10 stacks.
  • Red-glow attacks . Lethal unless a support shield or damage reduction covers you.
  • Fog . From x240 the arena edge is fog. Getting knocked in deals damage and cuts your sight.
  • Hand debuff . Hard and Nightmare. Certain attacks apply it, and it obscures your vision.

Gate 2 systems

  • Timed guards and grabs . JG and grab patterns run on a timer, not HP. After a long mech or a Rumble the boss can fire several back to back.
  • Late mechs . A mech whose line passes during the Rumble fires afterwards at lower HP. Do not read the HP bar right after a Rumble.
  • Late reinforcement . Late in the fight several patterns gain extra hits or flip their guard timing. Marked in amber in G2 patterns.

Coffin stagger

Memorizer 0:38 / Agam 0:52
  • Stagger
  • Purify

Three or four patterns after the opener, roughly 40 to 60 seconds in. Floor attacks come down and coffins appear, one on each side.

  1. Avoid the floor attacks.
  2. Get behind a coffin and stagger it. The cheat sheet stacks the whole raid behind the same one.
  3. The staggered coffin gives a safe zone. Stand in it.
  4. If you get crowd controlled, purify it.

Fails A failed stagger, or not being behind a coffin, stuns you.

Comes back About 5 minutes in, this returns as the ghost version further down.
x240cutscene

Coffin pizza and guards

Memorizer 1:28 / Agam 1:09 / Agam tips 0:00
  • JG
  • Counter
  • Ignores push immunity

Cutscene, then puddles fall (supports watch the party here). From now on the arena edge is fog. The first pizza set is purple lines that go through push immunity and add a debuff stack.

  1. After the cutscene, avoid the puddles and stand at 7 or 1 o'clock, near 5.
  2. JG toward the boss. Cue: about 2 seconds after the flash, as his wings gather. Then DPS.
  3. The 5 o'clock pizza always goes first. Once it explodes, move into 5 and keep hitting while 7, 11 and 1 fire in random order. The purple lines mark the slice about to explode.
  4. Second set: coffins fall on the diagonals in random directions, but the call order is fixed: counter, counter, boss JG, coffin JG, counter. The cheat sheet says the second pizza's random order sets the coffin order.
  5. The last counter ends the mech. Lines keep repeating throughout, so keep dodging.

Fails A failed guard or a pizza hit is +1 permanent stack. A failed coffin counter only deals damage, no stack.

Damage route Since the coffin counters only cost damage, Agam skips the first two: dodge the floor, keep DPSing under support cover, and only guard the boss and the guard coffin. Surviving is what counts here.
x195time attack

Yellow bar time attack

Wipe on fail Memorizer 2:24 / Agam 1:51 / Agam tips 5:00
  • Atropine
  • JG
  • Counter
  • Raid wipe

He vanishes, his HP locks, and coffins ring the arena. Then a yellow damage bar with a timer (about 1:08) appears under his HP. Burn the bar before the timer ends or the raid wipes. A cleared bar is worth about 35 HP bars.

A second debuff runs only here: every hit is -10% attack, and at 7 stacks you are stuck until the mech ends (Nightmare; on Hard it is -5% per hit and 10 stacks). Getting pushed into the wall adds a stack and pins you for a few seconds. Stacks are what make this mech fail, so dodge everything.

  1. Before x195, bank identity and hyper skill.
  2. Do not hit him the moment he reappears. Wait for the bar.
  3. Full combo plus Atropine at the bar start, while you have zero stacks.
  4. Play the cycle below until the bar is gone.

The patterns come in a fixed order, A then B then C, repeating. Within each letter the pattern is random.

A / Lasers

Waffle (twice), Cross, or One-way (twice). Move only after the beam right next to you fires. Lasers also paralyze, avoid them 100%. As they end, run to a corner (3, 6, 9 or 12).

B / Guard

He looks up with a yellow flash. Go to him and keep hitting. JG right before the white circle reaches the edge, as the yellow floor fills.

B / Corner

He looks up without a flash. The push hits everything but the 3, 6, 9 and 12 corners. Stand in a corner early, then leave it the instant the push ends, before the grab lands there.

C / Swing

Lines cross and he points with a white flash where the swing starts. It sweeps one whole side. Stand where his body faces, then step into the safe zone.

There is also a counter coffin variant: the attack covers the whole area, but countering the coffin makes a safe spot. A successful counter also removes one of your stacks.

Fails Timer runs out: raid wipe. 7 stacks: stuck until it ends. Caught by the corner grab: no stack, but lost damage time.

Shortcuts Hyper awakening can carry you through a B pattern. Push-immune classes can stand in the C safe zone with a push-immune skill and accept the single stack rather than lose uptime.
Sources differ Memorizer's card and the cheat sheet say x190, Agam says x195. The boss locks at x194 in Agam's pull, so the line is treated as x195. Memorizer lists the counter coffin with the A lasers, and the cheat sheet agrees (its A pool ends with a counter wall); Agam files it as a swing type.

Ghost coffins

Memorizer 0:54 / Agam 3:08
  • Stagger
  • Kill adds
  • Time Stop

The coffin stagger comes back reinforced, about 4:30 to 5:20 into the fight. Ghosts spawn from every direction and race to the center.

  1. Four coffins appear at 12, 3, 6 and 9. Take your 3x and 3x+1 positions, two players per coffin (12 and 1 at the 12 coffin, 3 and 4 at 3, and so on), and stagger fast.
  2. Kill the ghosts before they reach the center. Players get lines to aim at them: the red line kills a ghost, and lines come twice, so you get two chances.
  3. Dodge the floor attack that follows: Time Stop it, or go outside.

Fails Each ghost that arrives heals the boss about 3 bars and explodes for heavy damage plus a permanent stack. The floor hit is another stack. Red rings on the floor mean a coffin stagger failed.

Sources differ Memorizer staggers each coffin with two players. Agam's note suggests the coffin cannot be staggered and explodes once to summon the ghosts. The cheat sheet agrees with Memorizer: four coffins, two players each, stagger and then hide. Killing the ghosts is the part all three agree on.

Coffin hide

Memorizer 5:36 / Agam 3:34 / Agam tips 11:48
  • JG
  • Hide
  • Explosion kills

A stronger x240. He moves to the center and does the purple-line pizza again.

  1. Dodge the pizza and lines. The first pizza always starts at 9 o'clock.
  2. JG the coffin attack.
  3. Hug the back of one of the three coffins for the explosion.
  4. Dodge the lines again.
  5. Coffin JG, then boss JG (about 1 second after the flash) ends it.

x75clones

Three clones

Wipe on fail Memorizer 6:11 / Agam 4:07 / Agam tips 11:57
  • Synced stagger
  • 2-man co-op
  • Orb JG
  • Battle item

Cutscene: Clorian warns the clones are resonating and must all be cut at once. Three clones stand at 1, 6 and 11 o'clock in an upside-down triangle; the real boss is the one at 6.

  1. Split the raid into three groups. The two strongest staggerers take one clone, three players take each of the others.
  2. When the stagger bars appear, everyone throws the Serca or Belgardin item into the center. It hits all three clones.
  3. An alternating pizza runs the whole mech. It knocks down and slows stagger. Step into the slice that just fired.
  4. Stagger your clone at the same pace as the other groups.
  5. Take the real 2-man co-op counter. Some are fake, wait for the real one. A second co-op comes right after a red counter. The cheat sheet makes these 2-man co-ops on Nightmare only (normal counters on Normal and Hard), with fakes from Hard up.
  6. Orbs fly toward the center twice. JG the first as it closes in, move clockwise, JG the second.

Fails Timer runs out: raid wipe. Clones broken more than a few seconds apart: the stagger resets, effectively a wipe. A failed co-op counter resets stagger. An orb reaching the middle is raid-wipe damage; a missed orb can be body-blocked, but that player is imprisoned and needs freeing.

Priorities Agam rates the counters and orb guards above the stagger itself. None of them line up with the pizza timing.
Sources differ Sync window: within 5 seconds (Memorizer) or 8 seconds (Agam). Aim for 5. Memorizer puts the 2-player group on the bottom (6) clone; Agam's footage puts it at 1 o'clock. The cheat sheet agrees with Memorizer: party 1 takes the left clone, party 2 the right, and each party sends its best staggerer to the bottom. Pick one before the pull.

Gate 1 Rumble

Memorizer 7:58 / Agam 5:07 / Agam tips 3:41
  • Holding guard
  • 4-man co-op
  • Sword JG
  • Every battle item

A corridor damage race with a 3-segment bar and a countdown per segment. Damage carries into the boss HP. It showed up at x256 in one pull and x140 in another, so it is not tied to an HP line.

  1. Segment 1 locks until the guard. When the yellow snipe effect appears, immediately hold G facing him until it fills.
  2. Right after, the 4-man co-op counter. Success shows Perfect.
  3. Segment 2: throw everything, Serca or Belgardin item included.
  4. Segment 2 locks again until he holds a glowing sword. Tap JG (not held) about 1 to 2 seconds after the glow.

Fails A failed hold knocks you down and you miss the counter. A failed 4-man counter shows Miss and ends the Rumble.

Wait for x195 If the Rumble comes before x195, Agam resets: you spend the burst that x195 needs. If it keeps happening, stagger less or push more damage so x195 comes first.
Week one Low-gear parties will struggle to reach the third segment. That is expected.

G1 patterns

The regular rotation, grouped by what it asks of you. Pattern names are Memorizer's on-screen labels. Most hits knock down; red-glow hits kill without support cover.

Lethal

Red pizza One shot

Glows red, attacks twice. The third is a one-shot pizza that reveals the safe slice. Be in it.

Teleport grab JG

He vanishes and teleports to grab. While he catches someone he keeps grabbing. When nobody is caught, JG at his head, then get to his back: the follow-up one-shots.

If nobody lands the guard, Agam resets. Survivable with support cover.

Coffin beam One shot

Fall together: stand between the two coffins and DPS. Fall one at a time: the first one fires first. Stand at the second coffin, then cross to the first one's spot after its beam, or leave entirely.

Guards and counters

Holding guard Hold G

He turns to aggro and flashes with a yellow ring. Hold G right away, then one more JG that staggers him.

Guard and DPS JG

He dances and flashes yellow, JG immediately. Only players who landed the guard can shrink the red floor with damage. DPS open a safe spot and move in. The red floor is a permanent stack.

Run and counter 2-man

Big attack, then he runs across the arena. Close in avoiding the puddles (a stone hit catches you), then the 2-man co-op counter. Failing it is big damage plus a permanent stack.

Back step co-op 2-man

He suddenly steps back with a 2-man co-op counter. Hard to react to, so be ready. On a fail he dashes 2 to 3 times in a large range; the side hit is very dangerous.

Space and 3-man counter 3-man

Static effect. Spacebar as his cape rises and wings gather. Then a 3-man co-op counter. Miss the spacebar and you take damage, your mouse inverts, and you cannot counter.

Space and holding guard Hold G

Static effect. Spacebar on his sword swing. Success shows the guard direction; hold G toward it as he moves. If you missed the spacebar the telegraph is hidden from you, so players who succeeded ping the direction. The guard direction itself is not inverted.

Imprison and grab JG

Two pizzas that imprison, then a grab you JG about 1 second after the windup. Whether he moves sideways first decides which ring the grab hits.

Sources give opposite rings for each case. Watch the telegraph rather than trusting a rule.

Positioning and dodges

Triangle safe spot

Rotate through the triangle's safe spots in order. A laser hits the last corner. If he turns to the third spot early, the beam comes first: wait, then go in.

Triangle corner

Three side-attack lines come from outside. Stay inside first, then stand on the outer side of a corner.

Coffin swing

Coffins fall; he swings one three times in a wide range. His back is safe, but the third swing hits you if you hug him. A pizza follows.

Butterfly

Hits one side, then again. If anyone was hit by the first, he turns to them and hits everywhere except his back. Go to his back.

Side slash

Hits one entire side and the blade spreads. Stand exactly on the opposite side.

Velganus pizza

Uppercut, then a pizza at the aggro target for very high damage. His back is safe at the end.

Back and head

He turns to aggro, sucking in anyone at his back, hits his front, then a donut.

AoE barrage

A string of floor AoEs, about five (Agam sees four plus two that chase). Keep moving.

Boomerang

Throws blades in three directions, then teleports to aggro with a pizza. Stay off his head.

Back step pizza

Steps back for two pizzas, then sprinkles attacks on the aggro target.

X

Two front swipes, then he teleports and attacks in an X.

Sky drop

Goes up and lands on the aggro target. Aggro holder, take it away from the party.

Space

Coffins fall, he jumps, and a pulse goes out after he lands. Spacebar through it.

Gaze checks

Medusa Look away

Dark smoke under him: face away. A fail stuns, purify clears it.

Space and medusa Look at / away

Coffins appear and vanish while he moves with a puddle. Hits leave crowd-control circles, spacebar or purify out. A pulse comes out, spacebar it. At the end a ring on his back: shrinking, look at him. Growing, look away.

x360~1:15 timer

Orb destruction

Memorizer 0:34 / Agam 0:41
  • Counter
  • JG twice
  • Aim

Cutscene, boss HP locks, about 1:15 on the clock. Four orbs with orange HP (tentacles on the cheat sheet) sit at 12, 3, 6 and 9. Destroy all four. Two tools do most of the work.

Counter adds

Blue-diamond adds appear in pairs on the diagonals: 1 and 7, then 11 and 5, then 1 and 7. After a successful counter, a fan-shaped floor attack follows the counterer. Aim it over as many orbs as you can. It does not hurt players.

JG orbs

Appear at 12 and 6, then 3 and 9, then 12 and 6. JG once toward the inner side on the flash, then JG again aimed at the nearest orb. The JG orb moves toward your cursor on both guards. The first guard only primes it; the second, when it is smaller, deals the damage.

  1. Split the parties. Memorizer and the cheat sheet: party 1 left, party 2 right. Agam: party 1 at 12 then 9, party 2 at 6 then 3.
  2. One player per party takes each counter (Agam uses supports).
  3. Wave 1 aim (Memorizer): party 1 aims between 3 and 6; party 2 aims between the boss and 12 so its fans hit 6, 9 and 12.
  4. Wave 2 aim: party 1 aims down between 6 and 9; party 2 aims left of 3 to hit 9, 12 and 3.
  5. Dodge puddles, add only light DPS, and do not dump skills into nearly dead orbs.

With good aim and every guard landed, the orbs die in two waves. A long groggy follows: a very good damage window. If orbs still have a lot of HP after wave 2, hold skills, finish on wave 3, then burst the boss.

Fails A failed counter or failed JG hits everyone hard. Each extra wave costs berserk time.

Time Two waves instead of three saves about 20 seconds. The exact aim order is flexible as long as the fans land on orbs.
Sources differ Damage per hit: counter fan 20 to 25%, orb JG 30 to 33%, depending on the source; the cheat sheet says 20% and 30%. The fans from 1 and 5 reach up to three orbs; from 11 and 7 only two, so expect a little DPS.
x320~1:08 timer

Damage-reduction adds

Unlocks Belgardin item Memorizer 3:19 / Agam 1:38
  • Stagger twice
  • Purify
  • Battle items

Four big adds, many small adds, and a damage-reduction chain: small adds protect their big add, and each big add gives the boss about 25% reduction. Burn the boss's orange HP chunk before the timer ends.

  1. He spawns with a stagger check. Stagger fast. The floor silences, so purify or Bard-shield it.
  2. Party 1 to 11 o'clock, party 2 to 1 o'clock.
  3. Delete the small adds with battle items (Serca or Belgardin item, or a flame grenade); they die in about a second. They cannot be hit until the boss is staggered.
  4. Kill your big add before his second stagger. Only kill the two at 11 and 1. All four takes too long.
  5. From Hard up, a big add can tether you with a line. Get out of its range before it expires.
  6. Stagger him again and burn the chunk through the remaining 50% reduction.

Fails No chunk kill in time means no achievement and no Belgardin item. Staying in a tether locks you in place for 3 to 5 seconds.

Positions He moves during the mech but only appears at 9, 12 or 3, never 6. Most DPS can reach both big adds.
Sources differ Agam skips Atropine here because of the damage reduction. Memorizer calls it good during the chunk burn.
x280red field

Red field

Wipe beam Memorizer 5:55 / Agam 2:48 / Agam tips 6:17
  • JG
  • Counter
  • Support hyper
  • Raid wipe

At x300 he moves to the middle for a short cutscene. At x280 the arena turns red and he repeats JG then counter, three times. He has no head or back during this, and his stagger bar stays locked until someone counters.

  1. JG about 0.5 seconds after each flash.
  2. DPS between guards. Damage shrinks the red field.
  3. Any counter fires a 5 second beam over all the red that is left. Only counter when your tactic says so.
1 / Edge

Skip counters 1 and 2. DPS and drag him to an edge so a safe spot opens. Counter the third there and step in. Risky: he ends at the edge and the next patterns can knock you off.

2 / Middle, late

Keep him in the middle. Supports hyper awaken; someone counters the third. Everyone stacks, supports keep healing through the beam.

3 / Middle, early

Supports hyper awaken on the first guard; counter the first one in the middle. Unlocks stagger early and saves about 20 seconds. Most used in Korea, the route to two Rumbles, and the cheat sheet's recommended method. It needs about 5 seconds of heavy damage reduction.

Fails Anyone in leftover red without hyper awakening cover dies to the beam.

Potion timing Under support hyper awakening, drink only after about 4 seconds of the beam. The protection can run out early and you can still die.
Picking a tactic Use 1 or 2 if your support has no hyper awakening or the party is overgeared. Use 3 for low-gear parties (around 6.5k) and Broken Bone parties.

Angry Bird

Memorizer 10:18 / Agam 5:23
  • Stagger
  • Hold, aim, release

He teleports to one side and drops green puddles. Dodge them and wait in the middle. Launch circles appear; you slingshot yourself into him to stagger.

  1. Step into a launch circle.
  2. First ring ("keep holding"): press and hold left mouse when it turns red.
  3. Second ring ("aim, then release"): drag the aim so the landing circle covers the boss (pull to the side opposite him), then release when it turns red.
  4. He teleports once. Spinning purple beams imprison anyone they hit.
  5. If he is not staggered, a second and final round of circles comes after the purple beam ends.

Fails Releasing early, or getting hit by purple, imprisons you.

Red and red Act on the red both times. One jump each is enough to stagger, even with 7 players and no Gunlancer. Finishing on round 1 saves about 20 seconds; taking round 2 is fine while progging. About 5 seconds of free damage follow.

Gate 2 Rumble

Agam 6:12 / Memorizer 11:53
  • JG where he lands
  • Every battle item

A horizontal 3-step gauge replaces the boss bar, 10 seconds per step. All damage carries into the main bar, and reaching step 3 is worth about 70 to 80 bars. Agam's run hit it at x220.

  1. Burst step 1. Agam's raid pops Atropine together here.
  2. Between steps he signals left or right and a long or short jump. Run to where he lands and JG as he arrives.
  3. Step 2: use every battle item, Serca or Belgardin included.
  4. Chase and JG again, push into step 3.
Broken Bone Staggered-target bonuses apply at full uptime during the Rumble. If you cannot reach step 3, put a couple of players on Broken Bone plus Domination.
Second Rumble With enough time saved, a second one comes about 10 to 15 bars after Angry Bird. Reaching step 3 there usually kills him. That is the whole Rumble 2x plan.

G2 patterns

The regular rotation. Almost every hit is a push. Amber lines are the late reinforced versions.

Grabs

Only the rush grab is in the normal rotation; the others run on a timer and can stack up after a long mech.

Rush grab 2-man

Pulls back, shoots, charges forward (a plain push you can tank with push immunity), then sweeps a wide 270 degree grab. His back is safe. A 2-man co-op counter always follows, on the side where the sweep started.

Best: a push-immune move at his head as he strikes the ground. Otherwise slide behind him.

Front grab JG

Two tentacles down, then he spins. If he hits someone he grabs them toward aggro, then asks for a guard. JG right away to free them, and call it: a catch adds a follow-up attack.

Back hit and walk

Tentacles on his back; he hits his back, then grabs the area near him. If someone is caught he walks around and anyone the tentacles touch is grabbed too. If nobody is caught, two line attacks.

Clockwise grab JG

A long white and a purple tentacle stretch across the arena. The area near him is not safe. Go clockwise of the purple tentacle, keeping some distance. A JG follows about 0.5 seconds after the flash.

Late: he grabs twice. Go to the other side for the second.

Purple grab

Two big white hands, then a grab along the line of two purple tentacles. Stay away from the purple. Nobody caught: he hits twice. Someone caught: he blows away everyone near him.

Late: done twice.

Guards and counters

Hold into co-op Hold G

He suddenly asks for a holding guard. Hold G right away, then a 2-man co-op counter.

Hold into guard Hold G

He steps back. Hold G after the flash, then tap JG again immediately. Only players who landed the hold can take the second guard.

Green spot Hold G

A green spot appears and he moves to it. Everyone holds G as he slams it. The raid gets one grade by how many succeeded:

4 or more: Perfect, then a single counter. 1 to 3: Good or Great, then a 2-man co-op while he spins. Nobody: a wide-range hit.

Back hit, co-op, counter 2-man

Hits his back and steps back for a 2-man co-op counter. Then a 120 degree right-hand swing, then another counter.

Butterfly double guard JG x2

Butterfly shape and puddles. Two JGs: the first fast (guard toward him right away), the second slow (about 1.5 seconds after the flash). A failed guard can kill.

Late: slow first, then fast. Puddles or a red orb can catch you, which costs a big hit but skips the guard. At high HP that is an acceptable out.

Rain holes JG x2

Rain covers the field and everyone gets a debuff that deletes the ground under them after a few seconds. Spread so your hole does not land under others, stand on solid ground, then JG twice right away, or stand at his back. A failed guard can knock you into a hole.

Positioning and dodges

Four orbs Hole

Four orbs, then two 120 degree hits to his front. He drops the orb on the aggro side: a pulse, and the ground there breaks for a few seconds. Get off it. Aggro holder, do not pull the drop onto the group.

Bulldoze

Charges twice, then attacks. Purple: 270 degree hit, back safe. White: hits to his sides.

Late: three charges.

Squid

Long-hair shape, slams toward aggro twice with waves out the sides. Stay on his back.

Late: three slams.

Teleport

Drains energy in one direction, vanishes, reappears and attacks that way. Three variants: cross hit, back safe, front safe. Remember the first direction.

Wall

Summons a wall, hits toward aggro in a 150 degree cone, then hits his back. Either hit touching the wall explodes it. Aggro holder keeps him at 90 degrees to the wall.

Stone henge

Hand strikes raise stones. Two donut steps, then a beam out his sides while facing aggro. A beam touching a stone explodes over a big area. Stand at the stones; aggro holder faces him toward the henge.

Jump charge

Jumps and slams the aggro target; the area around the landing slowly turns to bombs. Go to his front or stick to his back, clear of the bombs.

Puddle shots

Shoots puddles at aggro twice, then slams front and back. His sides are safe.

Back tumble

Tumbles backward twice, then slams. Stay away, go back in after the slam.

Crab

Folds his arms, moves sideways, hits his front. Twice.

Front and back

Hits his front twice, then front and back slowly. Stick close, off both.

Swipes

Two front swipes, an outer-ring swipe, then a front slam. Stay close for the ring, leave his front for the slam.

Paralysis chain

First hit paralyzes, second knocks down, then a hit close around him and one on the outer area.

Stagger

Portal stagger Stop at 70%

He disappears into portals and a stagger bar appears. Staggering too early sets off a bomb. Stagger to about 70% and stop. Stand on a portal that has turned black (its beam silences anyone outside). When the green effect plays or his head lights up, finish the stagger.

Rumble 2x

Agam's Korean plan for low-gear Gate 2 parties: save enough time on mechs that the boss reaches a second Rumble before berserk. A step-3 finish there usually kills him and skips most of the late reinforced patterns.

Who it is for

  • Party combat power . About 6.5k to 7k, or 7k to 7.5k with weaker classes.
  • Build . About 2 players on Broken Bone plus Domination. It procs on every JG, every counter, the long groggy after x360, x320, x280, x240 and x100, and both Rumbles.
  • Everyone guards . Every player does every JG and counter, not only the assigned ones, to cover misses.

The payoff

  • Time saved . 1:00 to 1:30 of berserk across four mechs.
  • Rumble 2 . Comes about 10 to 15 bars after Angry Bird with about 70 to 80 bars left.
  • Two step-3 Rumbles . About 150 of the 400 bars. Expect berserk during the second one anyway.
  • Korea's default now . Agam says KR parties use these solves even when not going for two Rumbles.
